Licence

These icons are made publicly available for everyone to use freely,
without the need for attribution to the Commission or the AI Office.
However, signatories of the code of practice should use the icon in
accordance with its placement specifications. Usage of these icons by
non-signatories of the code should not be construed as signaling of
their adherence to the code.
